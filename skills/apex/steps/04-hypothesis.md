## Contract

Inline emit. `original_prompt` (verbatim; do NOT paraphrase) + `hypothesis` (1-2 sentences) + `complexity_hint: low|medium|high` + `alternatives: [{interpretation, status: kept|rejected, reason}]` (1-3 entries; minItems: 1; **`reason` MUST be one line, <=12 words** - rejection-code + short rationale, never multi-sentence; a 4-session cluster escalated verbose reasons; **display-verb alternative-coverage**: when `original_prompt` contains a display verb - `shows`, `displays`, `badge`, `banner`, `counter`, `pill` - paired with a data-mismatch complaint, at least one entry MUST be a client-side interpretation (e.g., `"server is correct; UI filter is wrong"`) alongside server-side ones, otherwise a UI-filter bug consumes a full server-trace budget before discovery; 36-tool-call server-side hypothesis on a prompt that said "shows the amount of refunded credits" where the actual bug was a UI accumulator filter) + `goals: [string]` (1..N items; each a discrete actionable check or task; free-text 1-3 sentences. Single-task prompts: one goal verbatim. Multi-task / audit prompts: enumerate one goal per discrete check). `goals.length` drives downstream behaviour at step 6 (top-K), step 7 (deterministic tier rule), step 8.2 (per-goal task split), step 13 (non-convergence detection), step 15 (per-goal summary). For `discovered_paths` only: when `original_prompt` has no path tokens AND `complexity_hint != "low"`, spawn a small Explore subagent (`Agent(subagent_type: "Explore")`) to gather candidate paths; the subagent returns ONLY validated repo-relative paths (existing on disk). **Pre-Explore ambiguity gate**: when the prompt has an "X is bugged / broken / wrong" shape and the bug-noun spans 2+ candidate repo surfaces (rough Glob of the noun across plausible dirs returns hits in distinct surface families), AskUserQuestion (`pick-surface-from-list | run-explore-anyway | abort`; dismiss = run-explore) BEFORE spawning Explore to surface the real surface at ~0 token cost rather than after a full Explore run + orchestrator context rewind (media-node MediaPreview vs shared useMediaInputList misdirect). Otherwise persist `discovered_paths` empty/absent. Write to `.claude-tmp/apex-active/{session}-hypothesis.json`; producer-validate via `bash skills/apex/scripts/validate-json.sh hypothesis.schema.json <path>`. Validation failure = abort with explicit error. **Hypothesis-write is mandatory under EVERY mode** - report-only, inline-skip, economy single-dispatch, and standard alike. The write is decoupled from executor dispatch: even when step 8 will skip the executor (`report-only`) or short-circuit on `already-satisfied`, the orchestrator MUST land hypothesis.json at this step so step-13 reflector reads a real `original_prompt` + `alternatives` signal rather than degrading to the MISSING fallback (report-only path silently skipped hypothesis.json; hypothesis.json absent at reflector spawn, non-convergence check degraded to no prompt_hash).
